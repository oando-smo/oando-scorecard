Report stuck on a sign in screen? Chrome and Edge fix

Chrome and Edge can block the cookies a framed Power BI report needs. Allow them once for this page:

Chrome

  1. Open chrome://settings/cookies in a new tab.
  2. Under Sites allowed to use third party cookies, choose Add.
  3. Enter oando-smo.github.io and tick Including third party cookies on this site, then Add.

Edge

  1. Open edge://settings/privacy in a new tab.
  2. Under Tracking prevention, open Exceptions and add oando-smo.github.io.
  3. Open edge://settings/content/cookies, and under Allow add [*.]powerbi.com and [*.]microsoftonline.com.

Then come back to this page and reload it. Browser pages such as chrome:// cannot be opened from a link, so copy the address into a new tab.
